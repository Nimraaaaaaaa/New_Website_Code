Questions before you book.

What do you actually build?

Automated profit and cash flow systems built in n8n, connected to your Google Sheets, QuickBooks or Xero, with dashboards in Google Sheets and Looker Studio and alerts straight to your inbox.

Do I need new software?

No. The Profit Clarity System runs on the Google Sheet or Excel file you already use. Offer 2 connects to your existing QuickBooks or Xero.

Is it safe to connect my accounting software?

Yes. We use read-only access, so nothing in your system can be modified or deleted. We show you exactly which permissions we request before we start.

What if we don't track hours?

Profit per client needs hours logged against clients. We cover this on the discovery call and help you set up simple tracking first if needed.

What happens after delivery?

Each system includes a support period for fixes. After that, the Operations Retainer keeps everything running and gives you a plain-English insights report every month.
